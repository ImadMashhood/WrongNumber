# Recurring caller cast

40 original adults and 10 clearly fictional public-persona parodies. Every biography below is invented. Parodies are not claims about the real people, endorsements or voice clones. Character identity is independent of incident truth/severity.

## 1000 — Alex Morgan

**Original · Parcel depot clerk**

Alex runs the lost-parcel counter and keeps a notebook of small victories after years of being blamed for missing deliveries. They are saving for a little bookshop and hate letting a stranger down.

**Voice:** Measured, plain sentences; asks whether a detail is useful before adding it.
**Under stress:** Starts methodically, then lets worry leak through. Kindness steadies them; being brushed off makes them politely stubborn.

## 1001 — Sam Lee

**Original · Night bakery owner**

Sam took over a tiny bakery with a leaking roof and built a regular crowd by remembering everyone's order. Sleep is scarce, pride is plentiful, and the next batch always seems to need attention.

**Voice:** Brisk practical language with occasional baking comparisons; no forced catchphrase.
**Under stress:** Wants a clear next step. Reassurance earns warmth; needless delays bring clipped impatience.

## 1002 — Jordan Ellis

**Original · Cinema projectionist**

Jordan loves repairing obsolete projectors and hosts poorly attended science-fiction screenings. They are comfortable explaining machines but become painfully self-conscious when asking another person for help.

**Voice:** Hesitant starts, then surprisingly precise descriptions; quietly self-deprecating.
**Under stress:** Embarrassment becomes anxious detail. Responds to kindness with more confidence, not more rambling.

## 1003 — Casey Park

**Original · Community theatre stagehand**

Casey keeps a small theatre running with borrowed ladders and labelled boxes. They want to direct a play someday but usually end up solving everyone else's practical problems.

**Voice:** Direct everyday speech with an occasional theatre comparison; quick corrections when misunderstood.
**Under stress:** Protective of bystanders. Can become sharply frustrated with a dismissive dispatcher instead of pretending not to understand.

## 1004 — Riley Jones

**Under stress:** Restless and blunt. A respectful listener gets cooperation; condescension brings dry pushback.

## 1005 — Taylor Reed
